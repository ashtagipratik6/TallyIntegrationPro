Post-Update Notice: "Failed to load Python DLL"

Temporary lock caused by Antivirus scanning immediately after an auto-update

Troubleshooting Tip
Error
✕
✕
Failed to load Python DLL
'C:\Users\...\AppData\Local\Temp\_MEI...\python314.dll'.
LoadLibrary: The specified module could not be found.
OK

Why this happens & how to solve:

When the app updates, your antivirus / Windows Defender briefly locks and scans newly extracted files (_MEIxxxx) in your temp directory. This is standard behavior and not an application bug.

  1. 1 Click OK on the pop-up error dialog to close it.
  2. 2 Wait a few seconds while your antivirus finishes marking the updated files as safe.
  3. 3 Re-open Tally Integration Pro. The application will launch updated and ready to use!
